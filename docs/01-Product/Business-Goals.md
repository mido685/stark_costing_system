# 1. Purpose

This document defines the strategic business goals of the Enterprise Costing
System. These goals guide product planning, feature prioritization, and future
development while ensuring the platform delivers measurable value to its
customers.

---

# 2. Primary Business Goals

## BG-01 – Improve Inventory Accuracy

Enable businesses to maintain accurate inventory records through real-time
inventory movements, stock counting, and inventory adjustments.

Expected Outcomes

- Reduce inventory discrepancies
- Improve stock visibility
- Minimize stock shortages

Success Indicators

- Inventory variance percentage
- Number of stock adjustments
- Stock count accuracy

---

## BG-02 – Reduce Food Cost

Provide accurate recipe costing using live ingredient prices.

Expected Outcomes

- Accurate menu pricing
- Better profit margins
- Reduced pricing errors

Success Indicators

- Recipe cost accuracy
- Gross profit margin
- Food cost percentage

---

## BG-03 – Improve Purchasing Efficiency

Digitize the purchasing workflow from request to supplier invoice.

Expected Outcomes

- Faster purchasing process
- Better supplier management
- Reduced purchasing mistakes

Success Indicators

- Purchase approval time
- Purchase order completion rate
- Supplier performance

---

## BG-04 – Improve Operational Visibility

Provide real-time dashboards and reports for managers.

Expected Outcomes

- Faster business decisions
- Better operational control

Success Indicators

- Dashboard usage
- Report generation frequency
- Decision turnaround time

---

## BG-05 – Reduce Manual Work

Replace spreadsheets and paper-based processes with digital workflows.

Expected Outcomes

- Less paperwork
- Fewer manual errors
- Faster daily operations

Success Indicators

- Percentage of digital transactions
- Manual correction rate

---

## BG-06 – Support Enterprise Growth

Allow businesses to manage multiple companies and branches from one platform.

Expected Outcomes

- Easy expansion
- Centralized management
- Consistent business processes

Success Indicators

- Companies onboarded
- Branches managed
- System performance under load

# 5. Long-Term Business Goals

The Enterprise Costing System aims to become a complete ERP platform for the
food service industry by continuously expanding its capabilities while
maintaining simplicity, reliability, and scalability.

Future objectives include:

- Mobile applications
- AI-powered demand forecasting
- Smart purchasing recommendations
- Business intelligence dashboards
- Predictive inventory management
- Multi-country support
