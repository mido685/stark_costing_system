**Document:** Module Breakdown
**Version:** 1.0

## 1. Purpose

This document breaks the system down into its major functional modules. For
each module it defines: what it's for, its key entities/screens, which
personas use it, and which other modules it depends on. This is the map of
the application's shape — Requirements defines the rules, this defines the
territory.

> **Note on scope:** two modules are included here beyond the original list
> (Production, Governance) because they already exist in the built system
> and in the Product Vision doc, and neither fits cleanly inside the other
> six. Flagged individually below — decide whether to keep them as their
> own modules or fold them into an existing one.

## 3. Inventory

**Purpose:** Single source of truth for what stock exists, where, and how it
moved.

**Key entities:** Ingredients/items, stock balances (per branch), inventory
movements (receipts, issues, transfers, adjustments), stock counts.

**Key screens/features:**
- Stock balance view (per branch, per item)
- Goods receipt
- Stock transfer between branches
- Stock adjustment (with mandatory reason code)
- Stock count / variance reconciliation

**Primary personas:** Storekeeper, Branch Manager

**Depends on:** Nothing — this is the foundation everything else reads from.

---

## 4. Purchasing

**Purpose:** Manage how ingredients and supplies get acquired.

**Key entities:** Purchase requests, purchase orders, suppliers, supplier
price history, cash purchases, petty cash, purchase invoices.

**Key screens/features:**
- Purchase request / purchase order creation
- Supplier registry and price history
- Cash purchase / petty cash entry
- Invoice upload and retrieval
- Delivery tracking against POs

**Primary personas:** Purchasing Officer, Branch Manager (approvals)

**Depends on:** Inventory (receipts feed stock balances), Governance
(price changes require approval before taking effect).

---

## 5. Production

**Purpose:** Convert raw ingredients into finished/semi-finished goods
according to recipes, and track that consumption accurately.

**Key entities:** Production orders, ingredient consumption records,
finished-goods output.

**Key screens/features:**
- Create production order tied to a recipe
- Confirm production (auto-consumes ingredients per recipe BOM)
- Record finished-goods output
- Actual vs. expected consumption (waste/deviation tracking)

**Primary personas:** Production Supervisor, Kitchen Manager

**Depends on:** Inventory (consumes stock, produces stock), Recipes
(defines what gets consumed and produced).

> **Flagged addition:** not in your 6-module list, but it's a distinct
> concern from Recipes (Recipes = definition, Production = execution) and
> from Inventory (Inventory = balances, Production = the transactions that
> change them via manufacturing rather than purchasing/sales).

---

## 6. Recipes

**Purpose:** Define what goes into a menu item or produced good, and
calculate its cost.

**Key entities:** Recipes, recipe versions, ingredient lines with quantities
and units, calculated recipe cost.

**Key screens/features:**
- Recipe builder (ingredients, quantities, units)
- Auto-calculated cost from current ingredient prices
- Recipe versioning (so past costing isn't retroactively changed by a recipe edit)
- Cost-change alerts

**Primary personas:** Kitchen Manager, Production Supervisor, Accountant

**Depends on:** Inventory (ingredient unit costs feed the recipe cost
calculation).

---

## 7. Sales

**Purpose:** Record what was sold and connect it back to cost/margin data.

**Key entities:** Sales transactions, menu items, linked recipe cost.

**Key screens/features:**
- Sales entry / import
- Gross margin calculation per menu item

**Primary personas:** Restaurant Owner, Accountant

**Depends on:** Recipes (to know the cost side of the margin calculation).

---

## 8. Reports

**Purpose:** Turn the data generated by every other module into decisions.

**Key entities:** P&L reports, budget vs. actual, cost trend, variance
reports, dashboards.

**Key screens/features:**
- Role-specific dashboard on login
- P&L per branch / per company
- Budget vs. actual
- Cost trend over time
- Variance reporting
- PDF export

**Primary personas:** Restaurant Owner, Accountant, Branch Manager

**Depends on:** Inventory, Purchasing, Production, Recipes, Sales — this
module reads from all of them, it doesn't own any data of its own.

---

## 9. Governance

**Purpose:** Enforce that sensitive changes (currently: supplier price
updates) don't take effect until someone with authority approves them.

**Key entities:** Approval requests, approval decisions.

**Key screens/features:**
- Pending approvals queue
- Approve/reject with logged decision

**Primary personas:** Branch Manager, Restaurant Owner (as approvers),
Purchasing Officer (as requester)

**Depends on:** Purchasing today; designed to be extensible to other
modules that need an approval gate later (e.g. large stock adjustments,
recipe cost overrides).

> **Flagged addition:** currently tucked conceptually under "Administration"
> in your list, but it's really its own workflow layer that sits *between*
> modules rather than configuring them — worth keeping separate so it can
> grow to gate other modules later without being buried in system settings.

---

## 10. Administration

**Purpose:** Configure and maintain the platform itself.

**Key entities:** Companies, branches, users, roles/permissions, system
settings, audit log.

**Key screens/features:**
- Company and branch management (including lifecycle: create/suspend/purge/delete)
- User and role management
- System settings
- Audit trail viewer

**Primary personas:** System Administrator

**Depends on:** Nothing functionally, but every other module depends on it
(tenant isolation, permissions, and settings all originate here).
